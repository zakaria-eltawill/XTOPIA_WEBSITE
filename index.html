Who are we?

XTOPIA is a Jeddah-based, community-focused business-building platform founded by Ali Al Shareef. We start with people — what communities genuinely need, and where today’s solutions fall short — then bring together entrepreneurs, operators and partners to build, launch and scale new concepts. Sometimes we create what’s missing. Sometimes we reimagine what already exists. Always, we build for lasting impact.
