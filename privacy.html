Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Registered company name] (“XTOPIA”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this website or contact us. We handle personal data in line with the Saudi Arabian Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations.

1. Who we are

[Registered company name], Commercial Registration No. [CR number], [registered address], Jeddah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

2. The personal data we collect

  • Enquiries. When you contact us by email or through the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the topic you select and the content of your message.
  • Job applications. When you apply for a role by email, we receive your name, contact details, your CV and any other information you choose to include.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider automatically processes limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data through this website. Please do not include it in your messages.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and continue the conversation you started with us.
  • To assess job applications and manage our recruitment process.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We only use personal data for the purpose it was collected for, or where the PDPL otherwise permits. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for direct marketing without your consent.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run third-party analytics. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only where needed: with XTOPIA businesses relevant to your enquiry or application, with service providers that host this website or provide our email services, and with authorities where the law requires it. Our service providers are required to protect your data and use it only on our instructions.

6. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia

Some of our service providers may process data outside the Kingdom. Where that happens, we take the steps required by the PDPL so that your data keeps an appropriate level of protection.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, and then delete or anonymise it. Enquiries are kept for as long as the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. Job applications are kept for the duration of the recruitment process and for up to [12] months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access your personal data, to request a copy of it, to ask us to correct or complete it, to ask us to destroy it when it is no longer needed, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

10. Children

This website is intended for a business audience and is not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.

12. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal data, email info@xtopia.life.
