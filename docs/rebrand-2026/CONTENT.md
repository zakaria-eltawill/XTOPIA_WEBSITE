## 9. XEED (p.29–32)

- Cover: *From seed to xeed*
- **Rooted in farming.** *Connected to markets.*
- An integrated agriculture business connecting farming, farm management and sourcing with market access.
  - Farming expertise — Growing capability from nursery to harvest.
  - Management & sourcing — Our farms, managed farms and a wider grower network.
  - Market access — Connecting supply to supermarkets and wholesale buyers.
- Stats: **53M+** developed farmlands (SQM) · **3,000+** employees · **50K** tons of produce per year · **3M+** trees across farmland
- **We grow. We manage. We source.** — Three ways to build supply. One connected route to market.
  - We grow — From nursery to harvest through our own farms
  - We manage — Operating farms for other owners
  - We source — Sourcing from a wide network of growers
  - One shared route to market: Sorting & packaging · Logistics & distribution · Supermarkets & wholesalers
  - *Market demand informs what we grow and source.*
- **Taking XEED further.** *Built in Egypt. Growing in Saudi Arabia.* ✅ approved
  - Three-year ambition: Build a substantial Saudi business across farming, farm management and sourcing.
  - Establish in Saudi — Adapt our accumulated farming expertise to local conditions and market needs.
  - Grow with farm owners — Build farm-management and sourcing partnerships that connect more growers to buyers.
  - Expand the offering — Broaden the product range and develop value-added processing capabilities.
  - Connect to customers — Strengthen logistics, distribution and supermarket relationships to support scale.
