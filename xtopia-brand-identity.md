## 1. Brand Overview

### Who We Are

**XTOPIA** is a next-generation creative ecosystem that brings together people, ideas, and opportunities under one roof. We curate and design environments that empower individuals and brands to collaborate, innovate, and thrive. From thoughtfully designed workspaces and lifestyle experiences to community-driven events and partnerships, everything we do is built to fuel meaningful connections and real impact.

Rooted in culture and driven by purpose, XTOPIA is more than a space — it's a movement toward a better way to work, create, and live together.

### Brand Statement

> *"We believe the future is built through connection. Not by working alone, but by creating together — bringing diverse minds, ideas, and energies into one ecosystem to shape meaningful experiences and lasting impact."*

### Tagline

**COMMUNITY · DRIVEN · PLATFORM**

## 2. Logo

### Primary Logo
The primary logo consists of the **XTOPIA wordmark** paired with the tagline **"COMMUNITY - DRIVEN PLATFORM"** beneath it, set in Acumin Variable Concept Extra-Light with wide tracking (309 pt). It is always used in its original colors on white or light backgrounds (off-white/cream text on dark background).

### Secondary Logo
The secondary logo is the **XTOPIA wordmark only** (without the tagline). Used when space is limited or in contexts where the tagline would be redundant.

### Symbol / Monogram
The standalone **X mark** can be used independently as a brand symbol. It may appear in any corner of a composition or centered.

### Logo Rules

- **Clear Space:** Always maintain a free margin around the logotype equal to the sufficient width of the wordmark. Nothing should be placed within this zone.
- **Letter Spacing:** The spacing between letters must always remain in its defined ratio — never alter kerning or tracking.
- **Minimum Size:** **3.5 cm** wide — this is the smallest the logo can be reproduced and remain legible.
- **Proportions:** Always maintain original proportions and colours. Never stretch, distort, or rearrange elements.
- **Print:** When embossed on print materials, use the same color as the paper.

### Logo Placement

| Preferred | Also Acceptable |
|-----------|----------------|
| Bottom left | Top right |
| Top left | Centered |
| | Centered left |

The symbol/X mark can appear in any corner or centered.

### Approved Color Combinations

The logo must only be applied using these approved combinations. Colors must never be altered, mixed, substituted, or rearranged outside these applications.

| Background | Logo Color |
|---|---|
| Off-White / Cream (`#F0EDE3`) | Dark / Black |
| Sage Green (`#C2C7A3`) | Dark / Black |

> ⚠️ Do **not** place the logo on a busy or low-contrast background. Ensure there is always sufficient background contrast.

## 4. Typography

### Primary Typeface — BARQUES

**BARQUES** is the primary brand typeface, used across all online and printed materials including envelopes, notebooks, and letterheads.

| Weight | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| Thin | Display / decorative |
| Light | Subheadings, supporting text |
| Regular | Body copy |
| Bold | Emphasis, headlines |
| ExtraBold | Strong callouts |
| Heavy | Maximum impact headings |

### Secondary Typeface — Book Antiqua

**Book Antiqua** is the secondary typeface and must only be used alongside BARQUES — **never on its own**.

| Style | Use Case |
|-------|----------|
| Thin (Italic) | Accent text, quotes, editorial contrast |
| Thin Bold (Italic) | Stronger accents alongside BARQUES |

> The combination of BARQUES (geometric, modern) with Book Antiqua (serif, editorial) creates a refined typographic contrast central to the brand's character.

### Descriptor / Tagline Typeface — Acumin Variable Concept

Used specifically for descriptors placed beneath the XTOPIA logo (e.g., "COMMUNITY - DRIVEN PLATFORM").

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Weight | Extra-Light |
| Tracking | 309 pt |
| Case | All Caps |

> This setting must always be maintained to preserve consistency, clarity, and the brand's refined visual identity.

## 5. Graphic Elements

### Brand Pattern / Grid
The brand's signature graphic element is a **subtle grid / cross-line pattern** — an abstract visual element derived from intersecting vertical and horizontal lines forming a grid. It must be used **minimally and selectively** as a background texture or supporting layer. It is never the focal point.

## 6. Photography Style

Photography is central to expressing the spirit of XTOPIA. The visual language is:

- **Human-centered** — people, community, real interactions
- **Atmosphere-driven** — authentic moments of creativity, connection, and collaboration
- **Warm & immersive** — natural environments, creative spaces, architecture, movement

### Guidelines

- Images should **never** feel overly staged, corporate, or artificially saturated.
- Color photography: tones must remain **soft, muted, and slightly desaturated** to maintain a refined, cohesive aesthetic.
- Both **natural color** and **muted monochromatic** treatments are acceptable — use to create balance and flexibility across the brand system.
- Subjects include: people collaborating, creative workspaces, architectural environments, close-up human emotion.


### Website Sections

| Section | Description |

### Key Web UI Details
- CTA style: uppercase spaced text + `→` arrow
- Form fields: minimal underline style (no bordered boxes)
- Grid-based layout with generous white space
- Monochromatic hero images with brand color overlays or overlay text
- Typography pairings: BARQUES uppercase for structure, Book Antiqua italic for warmth

## 9. Quick Reference — Do's & Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Use only approved logo color combinations
- Maintain the minimum logo size of 3.5 cm
- Keep clear space around the logo at all times
- Use BARQUES as the primary typeface across all materials
- Pair Book Antiqua only alongside BARQUES
- Use photography that feels natural, warm, and human
- Apply the grid pattern minimally and selectively

### ❌ Don't
- Stretch, distort, or alter the logo proportions
- Change logo colors outside of approved combinations
- Use Book Antiqua as a standalone typeface
- Use overly saturated, staged, or stock-feeling photography
- Crowd the logo with other elements in its clear zone
- Alter the tracking or letter spacing of the wordmark
- Use the logo below 3.5 cm in width
