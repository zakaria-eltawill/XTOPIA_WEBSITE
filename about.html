Who we are

WE ARE
XTOPIA.

XTOPIA is a Jeddah-based, community-focused business-building platform founded by Ali Al Shareef. We identify meaningful challenges in the way people live, work and connect — then build businesses designed to solve them, together with entrepreneurs who know their sectors from within.

Our story ↓

Our Origins

Our
Story.

We start with people — understanding what customers and communities genuinely need, and where the solutions available to them fall short. That's always where we begin.

From there, we bring together entrepreneurs who know their sectors from within, operators who can run at scale, and partners who open doors we couldn't open alone. Together, we shape a concept worth building — then we build it, launch it, and help it grow.

Sometimes we create what's missing. Sometimes we reimagine what already exists. Either way, every business we build is designed to stand on its own and to make the next one stronger.

"Sometimes we create what's missing. Sometimes we reimagine what already exists. Always, we build for lasting impact."
